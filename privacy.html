Zujaj ("the app") is a widgets app for Android made by SMRH. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. In short: no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and we never sell data.

Information that stays on your phone

  • Your widget settings, colours, texts, favourites and app preferences.
  • Photos, contacts and apps you choose to show in widgets. They are read only when you pick them and are never uploaded.
  • "Now playing" information (song title, artist, artwork, play state) when you allow music access for media widgets. Notification contents are not read or stored, and nothing leaves your phone.
  • Battery, storage and memory status shown by system widgets.

Location

If you allow it (or pick a city), your approximate location is used on the phone to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction, and it is sent — without any identifier — to the weather service MET Norway (api.met.no) to get the forecast. You can use the app without location by choosing a city manually.

Purchases (Zujaj Pro)

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details; the app only receives confirmation that a purchase is active. See Google's privacy policy.

Redeem codes

When you redeem a Pro code, the code, the time, an app-specific device identifier (Android ID) and the phone model are stored in our database (Google Firebase) so each code can be used only once per phone and its limits are respected. This information is used only for that purpose.

App configuration and security

  • The app reads public settings (which widgets are Pro or new, announcements) from Google Firebase Firestore.
  • Google Firebase App Check and Google Play Integrity may be used to confirm the request comes from the genuine app. Google processes this information under its own policies.

Anonymous usage counts

To understand how the app is used, Zujaj adds 1 to a few anonymous daily totals stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore): the app was opened today, a first install, which widget design was added, a free wallpaper was set, and a purchase was made. These totals contain no device ID, advertising ID, name, location or any content, and cannot be linked to you.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your phone. To ask us to delete a redeem-code record, email us with the code.

Third parties

  • Google Play Billing — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • MET Norway weather API — api.met.no/doc/TermsOfService

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
